# Tawseef Masud – Civil Engineering Design Portfolio

Three group design projects for St George's Field, University of Leeds (CIVE1665 Integrated Design Project, 2025/26):

1. **Raised Walkway** – an accessible cable-stayed walkway with weathering-steel pylons and a composite steel–concrete deck.
2. **Pavilion** – an outdoor shelter of 45 steel columns and tinted glass roof panels arranged, in plan, as St George's dragon.
3. **Vertical Barrier** – a replacement for a 20 m gap in the field's stone boundary wall, with foundation calculations.
